## Story
I brought this to a neighborhood potluck the first year we moved in, mostly because it was the only fancy-looking thing I knew how to make. It vanished in about nine minutes, and three people asked for the recipe before I had my coat off. It has been my "introduce myself to new people" dish ever since. It looks like effort; it is barely any.

## Ingredients
- 1 wheel of brie (8 to 12 oz)
- 1/3 cup honey
- 1/2 cup walnuts, roughly chopped
- 2 tablespoons dried cranberries
- A sprig of fresh rosemary, leaves chopped
- Crackers, sliced baguette, and apple slices, to serve

## Steps
1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Place the brie on a parchment-lined baking dish (or a small cast-iron skillet for drama).
2. Bake 12 to 15 minutes, until the center gives way when you press it gently — soft but not collapsed.
3. While it bakes, toast the walnuts in a dry pan until fragrant, about 4 minutes.
4. Move the warm brie to a serving board. Drizzle with honey, then pile on the walnuts, cranberries, and rosemary.
5. Serve immediately with crackers, bread, and apple slices for dipping and scooping.

## Make It Your Own
- For a gluten-free spread, serve with seed crackers, apple and pear slices, and cucumber rounds instead of bread.
- Swap the topping with the seasons: figs and balsamic in late summer, hot pepper jelly for a kick, or brown sugar and pecans for a dessert-y version.
- Wrap the whole wheel in puff pastry before baking if you want the showstopper version (not gluten-free).

## Notes
You can assemble the toppings ahead and bake the brie right as guests arrive. Warm cheese waits for no one — serve it the moment it hits the board.
