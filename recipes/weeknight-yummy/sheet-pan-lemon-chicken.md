## Story
This is the recipe I reach for on the nights when "Hey Mom, what's for dinner?" arrives and I have absolutely nothing planned. One pan, no fancy steps, and the oven does the work while I referee homework. It has saved more weeknights than I can count, and it tastes like I tried much harder than I did.

## Ingredients
- 6 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
- 1.5 lbs baby potatoes, halved
- 1 lemon (half juiced, half sliced into thin rounds)
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon salt
- Plenty of black pepper
- A handful of fresh parsley, chopped, to finish

## Steps
1. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
2. On a large sheet pan, toss the potatoes with 2 tablespoons oil, half the garlic, salt, and pepper. Push them to the edges.
3. Rub the chicken with the remaining oil, oregano, lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Nestle it skin-side up in the middle, tuck the lemon slices and remaining garlic around it.
4. Roast 35 to 40 minutes, until the chicken reads 175°F and the skin is crisp and the potatoes are tender.
5. Scatter parsley over the top and bring the whole pan to the table.

## Make It Your Own
- Swap the potatoes for any sturdy vegetable — carrots, fennel, green beans (add green beans in the last 15 minutes so they don't shrivel).
- Boneless thighs work too; just check them at 25 minutes.
- Make it Greek with a handful of olives and a crumble of feta at the end, or Moroccan with a teaspoon of cumin and a pinch of cinnamon.
- This is already gluten-free — serve it with crusty bread only if your table can have it.

## Notes
The lemon slices get a little crispy and caramelized in the oven. The kids fight over them. Make extra.
