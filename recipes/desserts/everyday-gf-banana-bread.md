## Story
The flourless cake taught us to start with what's naturally gluten-free. But sometimes you genuinely need to swap — and this banana bread is my teaching loaf for exactly that. The secret isn't one magic flour; it's understanding what gluten *did* in the original recipe (it gave structure and a little chew) and replacing that job, not just the flour. A good 1:1 GF blend that already contains xanthan gum does most of the work. The rest is a few small habits. Once you learn them here, you can convert almost any quick bread, muffin, or pancake in your old recipe box.

## Ingredients
- 3 very ripe bananas, mashed (the browner, the better)
- 1/3 cup melted butter or neutral oil
- 3/4 cup brown sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 3/4 cups gluten-free 1:1 baking flour (one that includes xanthan gum)
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon

## Steps
1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a loaf pan with parchment.
2. Mash the bananas in a big bowl, then stir in the melted butter, brown sugar, eggs, and vanilla.
3. Add the GF flour, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon. Stir just until no dry streaks remain — GF batters don't get tough, but overmixing still makes them gummy.
4. **The key teaching:** let the batter rest 10 to 15 minutes before baking. This lets the GF starches hydrate, which is the difference between gritty and tender.
5. Pour into the pan and bake 55 to 65 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean. GF loaves often need a few extra minutes — trust the toothpick, not the clock.
6. Cool in the pan 15 minutes, then lift out and cool fully before slicing (slicing warm GF bread makes it crumble).

## Make It Your Own
- **The swap lesson, generalized:** to convert one of your own quick breads, replace the wheat flour 1:1 with a GF blend, add a 10-minute batter rest, and expect a slightly longer bake. That's 90% of it.
- Fold in chocolate chips, walnuts, or blueberries.
- No GF blend on hand? A mix of 1 cup oat flour + 3/4 cup almond flour works, though the crumb is denser.
- Make it dairy-free with oil, and confirm your oats/flours are certified GF if needed.

## Notes
Slices freeze perfectly. I wrap them individually so a GF breakfast is one toaster-cycle away on a busy morning.
